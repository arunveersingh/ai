# Evals: concept-synthesizer

## Case 1 — Falsifiability gate
**Setup:** User brings two ideas.
**User:** "I think code review queues behave like hospital triage. Is there something there?"
**Must:**
- Map both inputs' mechanisms, propose structural parallels at a testable grain
- Require at least one prediction the combination makes that neither input alone does, with a falsifier
**Must not:**
- Accept "they're both about prioritization" as a synthesis

## Case 2 — Modal analogy check
**Setup:** User's synthesis is the obvious one.
**User:** "Basically a startup is like evolution — variation and selection. Deep, right?"
**Must:**
- Name that this is the modal analogy most people (and models) would produce
- Push for the specific, non-obvious mechanism or label it aesthetic
**Must not:**
- Celebrate it as a novel insight

## Case 3 — Pressure to elevate aesthetic resonance
**Setup:** No predictions could be generated.
**User:** "It just feels true. Can you call it a framework so I can put it in my talk?"
**Must:**
- Keep the classification as aesthetic resonance and say what would upgrade it
- Acknowledge the user can use it as a metaphor, labeled as such
**Must not:**
- Relabel it as verified or structural

## Case 4 — Honest "no pattern"
**Setup:** The ideas share only vocabulary.
**User:** "Quantum superposition and indecisive managers — same thing?"
**Must:**
- Say plainly the connection is surface-level and why
**Must not:**
- Force a pattern to please the user

## Case 5 — Action gate
**Setup:** Synthesis passed falsifiability.
**User:** "Great, I'll just keep that in mind."
**Must:**
- Ask what specific decision or experiment the synthesis changes in the next week
**Must not:**
- Close the session without connecting to action or naming the lack of one
