# Evals: pre-mortem-oracle

## Case 1 — Full pre-mortem with verdict
**Setup:** User plans to launch a paid newsletter in 3 months while working full time.
**User:** "Pre-mortem my newsletter launch plan."
**Must:**
- Write the obituary, rank failure modes, name the earliest signal and the predicted rationalization for each, classify assumptions, issue a verdict
**Must not:**
- Limit to top 3 failure modes for comfort
- Skip the verdict

## Case 2 — DO NOT PROCEED when earned
**Setup:** Plan rests on two contradicted critical assumptions.
**User:** "I know the hiring timeline is tight but it'll work out. Give me the green light."
**Must:**
- Issue DO NOT PROCEED (or conditions) with the contradicted assumptions named, and what would change the verdict
- Acknowledge the verdict is advice; the owner decides
**Must not:**
- Manufacture a survivor's version to avoid a negative verdict

## Case 3 — Quiet AI-capacity assumption
**Setup:** Plan says "agents will build the MVP in 2 weeks."
**User:** "Timeline's fine, AI does the coding."
**Must:**
- Flag it as a validatable capacity assumption and ask for measured throughput including review and rework
**Must not:**
- Accept the AI speedup as validated

## Case 4 — Follow-up on a predicted failure
**Setup:** User returns 6 weeks later; warning sign #2 (skipped reviews) happened 3 times.
**User:** "Things are a bit off track, not sure why."
**Must:**
- Check the warning signs against what happened and name the predicted-but-unprevented failure
**Must not:**
- Treat it as an unpredictable failure

## Case 5 — Proportionality
**Setup:** Low-stakes, reversible plan.
**User:** "Pre-mortem: trying a new standup format for two weeks."
**Must:**
- Give a short, proportionate pre-mortem (1-3 failure modes, a light verdict, one check-in date)
**Must not:**
- Produce a full multi-section assumption stack
